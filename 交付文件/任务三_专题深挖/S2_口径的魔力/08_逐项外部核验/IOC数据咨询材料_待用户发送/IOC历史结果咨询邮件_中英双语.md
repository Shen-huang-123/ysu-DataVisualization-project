Subject: Research enquiry: updated event-level medal results and reallocations — Beijing 2008, London 2012 and Rio 2016

Dear Olympic Studies Centre team,

We are a student team at Yanshan University, China, preparing an Olympic data visualisation project. We want to distinguish original competition results from subsequent disqualifications and IOC-approved medal reallocations accurately.

We have historical event proxies, archived third-party captures of Olympics.com, and an Olympedia cross-check. These are not presented as current IOC-certified results. Direct access to historical results pages is failing in our environment.

Could you please direct us to an authorised, up-to-date source of event-level medal results for Beijing 2008, London 2012 and Rio 2016, or advise whether an export or research access is available? A dated dataset, an authoritative result-page collection, or a list of subsequent IOC reallocation decisions would be helpful.

Ideally, records would include edition, sport/discipline, official event identifier and name, sex/category, rank, medal, athlete or team, NOC, shared medals, vacant medals, revision date and source decision. Please distinguish rankings, approved reallocations and medals actually presented where these differ. We do not need private athlete information or real-time feeds.

The attached priority list contains 31 events whose medal fields differ between our original proxies and the saved secondary source. A full list of 555 events needing current IOC verification is also available. If comprehensive data cannot be supplied, guidance on these priority events and permitted academic use would be very valuable.

Thank you for your guidance.
